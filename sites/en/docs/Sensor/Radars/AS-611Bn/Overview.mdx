# AS-611Bn Overview

The **AS-611Bn** is a 60 GHz pulsed radar module for single-target distance sensing or single-person vital-sign sensing. Its 32 × 30 mm board integrates the antenna, millimeter-wave integrated circuit, and digital signal processor. The module communicates through UART, and the AS-611Bn variant also provides Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE).

The product is available with separate distance-sensing and vital-sign firmware. Their command sets are different: a distance unit does not accept vital-sign commands, and a vital-sign unit does not accept distance commands. Check the unit label and firmware type before configuration.

## At a glance

| Item | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Radar | 60 GHz pulsed radar |
| Antenna | 1 TX, EIRP 10 dBm; 1 RX; gain 2 dBi |
| RF pattern at −3 dB | Horizontal 65°; vertical 55° |
| Supply / power | 3.3–7 VDC / 0.3 W |
| Operating / storage temperature | −40 to 85 °C / −55 to 125 °C |
| Board size | 32.0 × 30.0 × 3.35 mm without connector; 32.0 × 30.0 × 5.1 mm with connector |
| Interfaces | GPIO, 3.3 V TTL UART, BLE on AS-611Bn |
| Distance modes | 1 m, 2.5 m, 5 m, 7.5 m, and 10 m |
| Vital-sign range | Near: 0.15–0.5 m; far: 0.4–2.0 m; detection angle 70° |
| Vital-sign accuracy | More than 90% at steady state; respiration more than 95%; more than 90% below 1 m and more than 80% above 1 m |
| Vital-sign outputs | Breathing rate and waveform, heart rate and waveform, heart-rate variability (HRV) |
| Target profiles | Human, small dog up to 13 kg, large dog above 13 kg, and cat |

## Connectors

| Connector | Function |
| --- | --- |
| J1 | 2.54 mm pin header for power and both UART channels |
| J2 | 12505WR-06 six-position connector for power and UART 1 |
| J4 / J5 | USB-C power input; USB data is not available |

Any one of J1, J2, J4, or J5 can power the module. USB-C supplies power only, so read radar data through UART or BLE.

## Pinout

Pin 1 on J1 and J2 is identified by a square pad.

| Pin | Signal | J1 | J2 |
| ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | VCC | 3.3–7 V power | 3.3–7 V power |
| 2 | GND | Ground | Ground |
| 3 | GPIO | Input / output | Input / output |
| 4 | NRST | Not connected or high: normal; low: reset | Not connected or high: normal; low: reset |
| 5 | UART TX 1 | UART 1 transmit | UART transmit |
| 6 | UART RX 1 | UART 1 receive | UART receive |
| 7 | UART TX 2 | UART 2 transmit | Not used |
| 8 | UART RX 2 | UART 2 receive | Not used |

The board has four 3 mm mounting holes positioned 2.5 mm from the edges. Aim the face of the radar die at the target.
