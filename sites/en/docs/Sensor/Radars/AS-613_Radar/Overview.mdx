# AS-613 Radar Overview

The **AS-613** is a 60 GHz multi-channel frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) AI radar with one transmit and three receive antennas. It performs analysis on the module and supports multiple targets, contactless vital signs, fall detection, posture, presence, and position sensing.

AS-613 is the current flagship model, is in volume production, and is used for new hospital installations and conversions from AS-614.

## What it can sense

- Heart rate, breathing rate, and heart-rate variability using RMSSD and SDNN
- Respiration waveform and breathing anomalies
- AI fall detection and posture classification: lying, sitting, and standing
- Presence, occupancy, and vacancy for up to five people
- Distance and X/Y/Z position up to 8 m

## Antenna-in-package design

The BGT60CUTR13 uses an antenna-in-package design and cannot use a conventional shield can. Its package provides an equivalent three-dimensional Faraday structure. Ingenious holds a technical justification document, including an English appendix for overseas certification submissions. Request that document when a customer or test house asks about shielding.
