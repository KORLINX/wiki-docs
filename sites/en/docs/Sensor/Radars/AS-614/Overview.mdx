# AS-614 Overview

The **AS-614** is a 60 GHz frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) AI radar module with one transmit and four receive antennas. The board designation is **RB-614**. It performs AI analysis on the module for position, vital signs, presence, occupancy count, and hospital-bed monitoring.

AS-614 remains in production and is installed in existing InCare24 systems. New installations are moving to AS-613, while AS-614 development focuses on stability in hospital environments.

## MCU identification

The 2023 RB-614 v1.0 hardware folder identifies a GigaDevice GD32F303CBT6, while the current internal record identifies a Geehy AP32F411CE. The part may have changed between revisions, but that has not been confirmed. Do not quote the MCU to customers until engineering resolves the discrepancy. **[VERIFY]**
