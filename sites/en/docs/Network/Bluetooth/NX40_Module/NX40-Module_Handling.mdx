# NX40 Module Handling

NX40 is an LGA surface-mount module. Use ESD, moisture, placement, and thermal-process controls appropriate to the released production specification.

> **Production status:** The supplied draft does not contain sufficient module qualification evidence to publish MSL, floor-life, baking, reflow, and tape-and-reel values as approved NX40 production limits.

Before production, obtain the approved values for:

| Control | Required release information |
| --- | --- |
| Moisture sensitivity level | Approved NX40 MSL classification and governing standard |
| Shelf life | Duration and sealed storage temperature/humidity |
| Floor life | Time, temperature, and humidity after opening |
| Baking | Trigger, temperature, duration, tray/reel limitation, and maximum cycles |
| Packaging | Moisture barrier bag, desiccant, humidity indicator, and label content |

## Handling Guidelines

- Use an ESD-controlled workstation and grounded handling equipment.
- Keep modules in approved moisture-barrier packaging until assembly.
- Avoid touching or contaminating the LGA pads.
- Inspect the shield, antenna area or RF connector, package edges, and pads before placement.
- Pick up the module with an approved vacuum nozzle on the top surface.
- Avoid mechanical load on the shield, chip antenna, and external-antenna connector.
- Track the lot, package-open time, exposure condition, and bake history.

## Reflow Soldering

The diagram is reproduced from the complete review draft. Its `≤260°C` label remains a draft value until confirmed by NX40 module and assembly qualification.

The production reflow profile must be based on the approved NX40 assembly qualification and the final PCB thermal mass, paste, stencil, component density, and oven.

Do not use the nRF52840 package limit alone as the NX40 module reflow specification.

## Cleaning

Do not use ultrasonic cleaning or liquid immersion unless the process has been validated for the complete module. Confirm flux compatibility and residue requirements with the EMS/SMT process owner.

## Tape and Reel

Carrier-tape dimensions, pocket orientation, pitch, reel diameter, quantity per reel, leader/trailer lengths, cover-tape peel force, and label fields remain subject to the approved packing drawing.

## Production Checklist

| Check | Confirmation |
| --- | --- |
| Incoming material | Correct model, lot, packaging condition, and exposure history |
| Footprint | Released land pattern, orientation, courtyard, and paste aperture |
| Moisture control | Approved MSL, floor life, storage, and bake procedure |
| Thermal process | Approved profile and allowed cycle count |
| Placement | Qualified nozzle, force, position, and rotation |
| Inspection | Joint quality, alignment, shield, antenna/connector, and contamination |
| Traceability | Lot, oven profile, exposure time, rework, and inspection records |
