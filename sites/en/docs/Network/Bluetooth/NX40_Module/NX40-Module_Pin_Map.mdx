# NX40 Module Pin Map

The NX40 LGA interface exposes power, ground, USB, debug, GPIO, analog, NFC, and trace-capable signals from the nRF52840 platform.

> **Design review required:** The SoC ball names were checked against the nRF52840 product specification. The mapping from those balls to NX40 LGA pads is KORLINX design data and requires sign-off against the released PCB netlist and continuity test.

## Signals Not Routed to External LGA Pads

The supplied schematic identifies `P0.12`, `P0.15`, `P0.16`, `P0.24`, `P0.25`, `P0.26`, `P0.27`, `P1.01`, `P1.05`, `P1.06`, `P1.08`, and `P1.09` as not externally routed. RF, the 32 MHz crystal, and regulator-decoupling nodes are handled inside the module.

{/* Confirm the non-routed list against the released PCB for all four NX40 variants. Omitting the additional Flash on a `00` model does not make its internal Flash nets external LGA signals unless the released layout explicitly routes them. */}

## Integration Notes

- Choose the approved VDD or VDDH topology; do not drive conflicting supply rails.
- Treat `DCC`, `DCCH`, `DECUSB`, and other `DEC` nodes as regulator or decoupling nodes.
- Route `D+` and `D−` as a 90 Ω differential pair with minimal stubs.
- Expose `SWDIO`, `SWDCLK`, `nRESET`, and ground in the programming fixture.
- Configure P0.00/XL1 and P0.01/XL2 consistently with the external 32.768 kHz crystal decision.

### External QSPI Planning

For a separate host-connected QSPI device, Nordic recommends `P0.18/nRESET` for CSN, `P0.19` for SCK, and `P0.21`, `P0.22`, `P0.23`, and `P1.00/SWO` for data on the WLCSP package. Pin selection remains firmware-configurable. Keep this interface separate from the module-internal Flash connections on `32` variants.
