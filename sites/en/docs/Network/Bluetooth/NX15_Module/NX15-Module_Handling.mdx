# NX15 Module Handling

This page summarizes important handling, packing, storage, and soldering notes for the **KORLINX NX15 Series BLE module**.

NX15 is an LGA surface-mount module and should be handled using proper ESD, moisture, and reflow process controls.

:::caution
This page is a Wiki summary for handling reference. For production assembly, always follow the official datasheet, approved manufacturing process, and EMS/SMT process requirements.
:::

## Storage Conditions

| Condition | Specification |
| --- | --- |
| Storage Temperature | 5°C to 35°C, unopened |
| Storage Humidity | 20% to 70% RH, unopened |
| Shelf Life | 12 months from shipment date |
| MSL Level | MSL 3 (IPC/JEDEC J-STD-020) |
| Floor Life | ≤168 hours at &lt;30°C / &lt;60% RH |
| Baking | 125°C +5/-0°C, 24 hours, one time max |
| Corrosive Atmosphere | Avoid Cl₂, NH₃, SO₂, or NOx |

---

## Handling Guidelines

Follow these guidelines when handling NX15 modules:

- Use proper ESD protection.
- Avoid touching LGA pads with bare hands.
- Inspect modules for cracks, shield damage, contamination, or pad damage before assembly.
- Keep modules in the moisture barrier bag until assembly.
- Track floor life after opening the package.
- Use vacuum nozzle pickup on the module top surface only.
- Do not apply mechanical stress to the shield, RF connector, or antenna area.

| Zone | Temperature | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Preheat | 25°C to 150°C | 60 to 120 s |
| Soak | 150°C to 200°C | 60 to 120 s |
| Reflow Peak | ≤260°C | Max 30 s above 250°C |
| Cooling | 260°C to 25°C | &lt;6°C/s ramp-down |
| Number of Reflows | - | Maximum 2 times |

:::caution
Do not exceed the recommended reflow peak temperature or number of reflows. Excessive thermal stress may damage the module or degrade RF performance.
:::

## Cleaning Notice

Cleaning is not recommended.

The NX15 module is moisture-sensitive. Ultrasonic cleaning or liquid immersion may damage internal components or degrade RF performance.

If cleaning is required by the production process, use only a method approved for the final manufacturing process.
